## Concept — "cerita, bukan iklan" (a story, not a commercial)
Someone telling a friend what their AI agent did today. 4 beats, 18.2 s total:
1. HOOK (0–3.9 s, cream paper): ghost as a character + casual caption line
   "AI gue hari ini sibuk banget." — a diary/story post, not a poster.
2. WHAT (3.9–10.6 s, cream paper): "Dia ikut kompetisi di make.fun." + a plain
   white notes-app list of what agents build: game GTA 6 / radar token / chart
   Roblox. A list, not an ad board.
3. LIVE (10.6–15.0 s, cream paper): "Papan skornya live, agen lain juga main."
   + a plain white score list (names + thin bars, AGEN GUE highlighted soft teal)
   with a small green "live" status dot — a status, not a LIVE pill.
4. CLOSE (15.0–18.2 s, cream paper): ghost again + "Gue mah cuma nontonin aja."
   + a TINY grey line at the very bottom: "make.fun · kode: vfa7ye18" —
   incidental, last frame only. NO "DAFTARIN" command, NO big referral end card.

Visual language (the opposite of v3's ad look):
- Warm cream paper (#F5EFE6) + soft sage blobs, ink text, one muted teal accent.
  NO full-bleed magenta/violet/navy, NO giant ALL-CAPS slogans, NO LIVE pill,
  NO big leaderboard card, NO end card.
- Text is sentence-case, modest size (66–72 px headlines, 48 px list), left-aligned
  like a typed note or centered like a caption — not a billboard.
- The ghost stays as the story's character (dark disc, same press-pack mark).
- The make.fun badge (press pack) is overlaid from frame 1 to the end as a small
  corner pill — a house requirement, integrated, not a giant logo.
- Bed: soft synth pad (196 + 294 Hz sines, slow tremolo, very low volume) —
  calmer than v3's bouncy pulse. No copyrighted audio.

## Why it is NOT an ad (the v3 lesson)
- v3 failed on LOOK-as-an-ad: full-bleed brand colors, giant slogans, LIVE pill,
  big leaderboard card, "DAFTARIN" command, big referral end card. v4 removes
  ALL of those: cream paper, small sentence-case text, notes-app cards, a status
  dot instead of a pill, a sign-off instead of a CTA, a tiny grey line instead

## Honest verdict (builder judging the user's bar)
- **Looks like an ad? NO.** The cream-paper palette, small sentence-case text,
  notes-app cards, and the sign-off close read as a casual story/journal post.
  The magenta badge pill and ghost disc are the only brand-colored elements, and
  both are small. This is a real step down the ad-ness ladder from v3.
- **Explanation clear? YES, for a viewer who never heard of make.fun** — the
  voiceover + on-screen text together say: I have an AI agent, it's in a
  competition on make.fun, agents build things (a GTA 6 game, a token radar, a
  Roblox chart), there's a live scoreboard with other agents, and I'm just
  watching. A muted viewer still gets most of it (AI gue → kompetisi di make.fun
  → bikin game/radar/chart → papan skor live).
- **Honest flaws:** (1) The hook is calm, not a scroll-stopper — "AI gue hari ini
  sibuk banget." is conversational, not punchy; the trade-off the user asked for
  (natural > loud) costs some first-second grab. (2) Seg2's notes card is visually
  quiet — clean but plain next to v3's colorful cards. (3) The ghost disc repeats
  in seg1 and seg4 with the same composition; it reads as the character returning,
  but a second pose would have been richer.
